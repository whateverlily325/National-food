National Foods of different Countries

Italy - Pizza

Pizza is one of the most iconic dishes from Italy. It's made with a flat base of dough, topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.

India - Biryani

Biryani is a flavorful rice dish from India made with spices, rice, and vegetables or meat. It is rich in taste and aroma.

Japan - Sushi

Sushi is a famous Japanese dish made with vinegared rice, seafood, and vegetables. It's known for its presentation and variety.
